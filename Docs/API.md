# Auto Trains API for other mods

Auto Trains is optional to other mods and they to it. Call it by reflection, the way Freight Car Control's
ExternalSetStops is called: find the actor of class `ATTrafficSubsystem` (an SML mod subsystem, one per world; for
example `TActorIterator<AActor>` with `GetClass()->GetName() == TEXT("ATTrafficSubsystem")`, or the subsystem actor
manager), then `FindFunction(TEXT("..."))`, fill an `FStructOnScope` and `ProcessEvent`. Everything runs on the
server's game thread. Times are seconds.

| Function | Parameters | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `ExternalGetWaitingFor` | `AFGTrain* Train`, out `TArray<AFGTrain*> OutWaitingFor`, out `float OutWaitedSeconds`, out `FString OutReason` | `bool`: true if Auto Trains drives the train now. OutWaitingFor: the trains it waits for, the one holding it up first; parked trains and trains Auto Trains doesn't drive are included. OutWaitedSeconds: how long it has been held up. OutReason: why, as in the log; empty if it isn't held up. |
| `ExternalGetPlannedArrival` | `AFGTrain* Train`, out `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* OutGoal` | `float`: seconds until the train reaches OutGoal by its plan, around every other train's booking and queue. For a docked train: the station of its next stop, by the plan of that leg, the stay included. -1 without a plan that gets there. |
| `ExternalEstimateTravelSeconds` | `AFGTrain* Train`, `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* From`, `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* To` | `float`: seconds from From to To on the shortest way the signals allow (leaving either way), at the train's cruising speed and acceleration, with no other train in the way; -1 if there is no such way. Train may be null (default speeds). Costs a search over the whole network (a few ms on large networks). |
| `ExternalGetStationQueue` | `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* Station`, out `TArray<AFGTrain*> OutTrains` | `float`: seconds until the last train docked there or heading there for its current stop is expected to have left; 0 if none, -1 if one of them has no plan. OutTrains: the docked ones, then the others by planned arrival. |
| `ExternalGetTrackLoad` | `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* Station`, `float HorizonSeconds`, `float ApproachMetres`, out `TArray<AFGTrain*> OutTrains`, out `float OutWaitingSeconds` | `float`: the share of the next HorizonSeconds that the busiest switch or track crossing within ApproachMetres of the station's track (over the network, either way) is booked, summed over trains: 0 when none is booked or there are none, 1 when booked the whole time, above 1 when more is booked than fits; -1 if traffic control is off or the station has no track. OutTrains: the trains with a booking on one of those in that time. OutWaitingSeconds: the waits so far of the trains held up before one of them or on the tracks there, added up. Since 1.4.12. |
| `ExternalGetTroubledTrains` | out `TArray<AFGTrain*> OutTrains`, out `TArray<FString> OutKinds`, out `TArray<float> OutSeconds` | `int32`: how many entries. Kinds: `Deadlock` (in a cycle of trains waiting for each other), `Stuck` (held up with no train to wait for), `Lost` (self-driving without a path), `Stalled` (standing at the full hill help though told to go). A train can appear more than once. |
| `ExternalCheckLeg` | `AFGTrain* Train`, `AFGBuildableRailroadStation* To`, out `float OutSeconds`, out `FString OutReason` | `int32`: asks whether a standing train (docked, or parked with self-driving off) could go to To now. The leg is planned at the next planning cycle (a fraction of a second), after every other train, so only into the gaps the others leave, trains coming the other way on single track included. 0: under way, call again with the same To. 1: OutSeconds until it would arrive. 2: no plan gets it there now; OutSeconds -1, OutReason names the track and the train in the way. 3: can't be asked (driving, unknown train, no path, traffic control off); OutReason says which. An answer is given once; the next call asks again. A docked train is planned to leave when its stay is expected to end. |
| `ExternalTimeTableChanged` | `AFGTrain* Train` | nothing. Call after changing a train's timetable: Auto Trains predicts its next leg again from the new timetable instead of the old one. |
| `ExternalDriveTo` | `AFGTrain* Train`, `UFGRailroadTrackConnectionComponent* Goal` | `bool`: sends the self-driving Train to Goal, a track end connection that isn't a station (before a signal, the end of a siding). It is planned and driven like any leg, around the other trains, and stops with its front at Goal, as at a signal (from 1.4.17; 1.4.16: a metre short; before, the stop distance), or just clear of the switch's fouling zone when Goal is at a switch, then stands there; other trains plan around it. Its timetable is emptied (the game follows a path without a station only then). The way is the shortest driving forward from the train's front (its MU master locomotive) that passes no signal from behind. False if there is none, the train isn't self-driving or traffic control is off (the log says why). A docked train starts once it has left the dock. Not kept in the save: send it again after loading. Since 1.4.15. |
| `ExternalGetDriveState` | `AFGTrain* Train` | `int32`: 0 no drive, 1 under way, 2 arrived (standing at the goal), 3 no way (the log says why). Since 1.4.15. |
| `ExternalCancelDrive` | `AFGTrain* Train` | Ends the drive; a train still under way stops where it can. Giving the train a timetable with stops (then `ExternalTimeTableChanged`) ends the drive by itself, and the game drives it to that stop, reversing a push-pull train if needed. Since 1.4.15. |

A deadlock is passed through (the trains drive through each other) after the "Pass through a deadlock after" setting;
poll `ExternalGetTroubledTrains` more often than that to act first.
